Tested on 121 of this season's games (Oct 24 to Nov 9; a team's first two games of the season are left out) with lessons learned only from the 3,586 games of the three seasons before, so the lessons had never seen these games. What to build in this version was chosen by studying last season's games and checked against these 121, so read the gains as a careful estimate, not a blind test. Each game was replayed with what the live app knows at that moment: season stats to date, team ratings, the closing line and the lineup list as it stands at tip-off. Average miss counts everyone who really played 10+ minutes, and a player the app had no number for counts as a full miss. Box scores count every made three (see the first finding), for "before" and now alike. "Before" is the app as it was on Nov 10, taught on the same games. After this test the app went on to learn from these games as well, as it does from every finished game.
