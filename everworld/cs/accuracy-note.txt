Tested on this season's 121 complete games (Oct 21 to Nov 9) with lessons learned only from the 3,586 games of the three seasons before, so the app had never seen these games. Each game was replayed with what the live app knows at that moment: season stats to date, team ratings, the closing line and the lineup list as it stands at tip-off. Average miss counts everyone who really played 10+ minutes, and a player the app had no number for counts as a full miss. "Before" is the app as it was on Nov 10, taught on the same games. After this test the app went on to learn from these games as well, as it does from every finished game.
