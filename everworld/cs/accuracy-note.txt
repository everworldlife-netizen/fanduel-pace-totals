Tested on this season's 123 games (Oct 21 to Nov 9) with lessons learned only from the 3,586 games of the three seasons before, so the app had never seen these games. Each game was replayed with what the live app knows at that moment: season stats to date, team ratings, the closing line and the posted lineup. Average miss counts everyone who really played 10+ minutes, and a player the app had no number for counts as a full miss. "Before" is the app as it was on Nov 10, taught on the same three seasons.
