The edge is the call

Edge = GG projection − FanDuel’s line. A plus edge means our number is ahead of FanDuel’s, so we expect more points than FanDuel does. We call it when we’re 2+ points ahead and the price still pays, and sit out the final minutes. Below the line is never a call.
